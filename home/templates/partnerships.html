Partner With Us

Together, we can build a brighter future for our students and our community. Explore partnership opportunities with Kasubi Parents Secondary School.

Why Partner With Us

Stronger Together — Building Brighter Futures

At Kasubi Parents Secondary School, we believe that quality education is a shared responsibility. We partner with individuals, organizations, businesses, and institutions who share our vision of empowering young people through education.

Whether you are an NGO looking to support education, a business interested in community development, an individual wanting to sponsor a student, or an institution seeking collaboration — there are many ways to partner with us.

Every partnership, big or small, makes a real difference in the lives of our students and the future of our community.

Partnership Opportunities

Ways You Can Partner With Us

Choose the partnership model that aligns with your mission, values, and goals.

Student Sponsorship

Sponsor one or more students who need financial support to access quality secondary education.

  • Full or partial scholarship
  • Tuition, boarding & scholastic materials
  • Progress reports to sponsors

Infrastructure Support

Contribute to the development and improvement of our school facilities and learning environment.

  • Classroom & lab construction
  • Library & ICT development
  • Dormitory & dining improvements

Academic Collaboration

Collaborate with us on academic programs, teacher training, and curriculum development.

  • Teacher development programs
  • Curriculum support & resources
  • Research & innovation projects

Materials & Resources

Donate textbooks, laboratory equipment, computers, and other essential learning materials.

  • Textbooks & reference materials
  • Science lab equipment
  • Computers & ICT supplies

Mentorship & Skills

Share your expertise through career talks, mentorship programs, and skills training for students.

  • Career guidance sessions
  • Life skills workshops
  • Entrepreneurship training

Community Development

Partner with us on community outreach programs that extend education beyond our school walls.

  • Community literacy programs
  • Health & wellness initiatives
  • Environmental projects
Mutual Benefits

What Our Partners Gain

We value our partners and are committed to building relationships that are meaningful, transparent, and mutually beneficial.

Recognition

Public recognition of your contribution on our website, school events, and publications.

Impact Reports

Regular updates and reports showing the direct impact of your partnership on students.

Community Goodwill

Strengthen your brand's reputation as a responsible and community-focused organization.

Networking

Connect with other partners, educators, and community leaders at our events and forums.
