How It Works

Our Admission Process

Four simple steps to join the Kasubi Parents family.

1

Submit Application

Fill out the online application form below, or visit our school office to pick up a physical form.

2

Submit Documents

Provide the required documents — previous school reports, PLE results (for S1), and a birth certificate.

3

Interview & Assessment

Attend a short interview and academic assessment, and receive a school tour if you are visiting.

4

Offer & Enrollment

Receive an admission offer, complete payment, and officially enroll your child at Kasubi Parents.

What You'll Need

Admission Requirements

Please prepare the following documents before applying.

For Senior One (S1)

New students joining our O-Level program.

  • PLE Results Slip — original and copy
  • Primary School Report Cards — from P5 to P7
  • Birth Certificate or copy
  • Passport Photos — 2 recent copies
  • Recommendation Letter from previous school
  • Completed Application Form

For Transfer Students

Students joining S2 – S6 from another school.

  • Transfer Letter from previous school
  • Recent Report Card — most recent term
  • Academic Transcript (for A-Level transfers)
  • Birth Certificate or copy
  • Passport Photos — 2 recent copies
  • Completed Application Form

Admission FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about admissions at Kasubi Parents Secondary School.

Admissions are open throughout the year, but we encourage applications well before the start of each term. For the main intake (Senior One), we recommend applying immediately after PLE results are released.

Yes, we conduct a short academic assessment for all applicants. This helps us place students appropriately and understand their learning needs. The assessment is friendly and not intended to be stressful.

We have a limited number of scholarships and bursaries available for students with exceptional academic ability or genuine financial need. These are awarded through our partners. Please contact the school office for more information.

In special circumstances, we do consider mid-term admissions — especially for transfer students. Please contact our admissions office to discuss your situation, and we will guide you accordingly.

Fees are typically payable at the beginning of each term. We offer flexible payment plans for families who need them. Please speak to our bursar for details on payment schedules.

Once your application is reviewed, we will contact you by phone and email within 2–3 working days to inform you of the outcome and next steps. Successful applicants will receive an admission letter.
