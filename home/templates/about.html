Our Story

Building a Legacy of Excellence Since Day One

Kasubi Parents Secondary School was founded with a simple yet powerful vision — to create a school where every child is given the opportunity to shine. What began as a small community school has grown into a thriving day and boarding secondary school, serving students from Senior One to Senior Six.

Located along Nabulagala Road in the Rubaga Division of Kampala, our school sits in a peaceful and accessible environment that is ideal for focused learning. Over the years, we have remained committed to providing a well-rounded education that balances academic excellence with character formation.

Today, Kasubi Parents Secondary School stands as a place where students are not only prepared for national examinations but are also equipped with the values, skills, and confidence they need to succeed in life beyond the classroom.

Licensed by the Ministry of Education and Sports
Our Direction

Vision & Mission

The guiding principles that shape everything we do at Kasubi Parents Secondary School.

Our Vision

To be a leading secondary school that nurtures confident, disciplined, and academically excellent learners — empowering them to become responsible leaders and positive contributors to society. We envision a community where every student discovers their God-given potential and uses it to transform the world around them.

Our Mission

To provide quality, holistic secondary education in a safe and caring environment — through dedicated teaching, strong moral values, and a supportive community. We are committed to helping every student achieve academic success, develop good character, and grow into a lifelong learner who serves others with integrity and compassion.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

The principles that guide our students, teachers, and entire school community every day.

Excellence

We pursue excellence in academics, character, and co-curricular activities — always striving to do our best and improve every day.

Integrity

We teach and model honesty, accountability, and strong moral principles — shaping students who do the right thing even when no one is watching.

Compassion

We foster a culture of kindness, empathy, and respect — where every student feels valued, supported, and encouraged to care for others.

Community

We build strong partnerships between students, parents, teachers, and the wider community — because education is a shared responsibility.

Lifelong Learning

We inspire curiosity and a love for learning that extends beyond the classroom — preparing students for a lifetime of growth and discovery.

Discipline

We believe that self-discipline and responsibility are essential foundations for success — in school, at home, and in the wider world.
