Welcome to Kasubi Parents

Nurturing Confident Learners & Future Leaders

At Kasubi Parents Secondary School, we provide a nurturing foundation for confident learners. Our school is a compassionate community built on a simple, child-centered approach that supports both academic learning and character growth.

Located along Nabulagala Road in the Rubaga Division of Kampala, we guide our students from Senior One to Senior Six through a transformative journey — equipping them with the knowledge, skills, and values they need to thrive in a rapidly changing world.

Our Academics

O-Level & A-Level Excellence

We offer a comprehensive secondary education designed to prepare students for UNEB examinations and beyond.

O-Level (S1 – S4)

Our O-Level program provides a strong academic foundation across the sciences, arts, and humanities — preparing students for success in their UCE examinations.

  • Sciences: Biology, Chemistry, Physics
  • Mathematics & ICT
  • Languages: English, Literature, Luganda
  • Humanities: History, Geography, CRE
  • Business & Technical Subjects

A-Level (S5 – S6)

Our A-Level program offers specialized subject combinations that prepare students for university education and professional careers.

  • Sciences: PCB, PCM, BCM, PEM
  • Arts: HEG, HEL, HED, EDP
  • Subsidiary ICT & General Paper
  • Career Guidance & University Prep
  • Academic Mentorship Programs
Student Life

Day & Boarding Options

Whether your child joins us as a day scholar or a boarder, they become part of a supportive community that feels like home.

Day Section

Our day section offers a vibrant, structured learning environment for students who live nearby. Day scholars enjoy full access to all academic and co-curricular programs.

  • Structured daily timetable
  • Access to all facilities & clubs
  • Hot lunch program available
  • Safe drop-off and pick-up routines

Boarding Section

Our boarding section provides a secure, disciplined, and nurturing home away from home — with supervised study, balanced meals, and a supportive community.

  • Supervised evening & morning prep
  • Clean, spacious dormitories
  • Balanced, nutritious meals
  • 24/7 matron & security presence
