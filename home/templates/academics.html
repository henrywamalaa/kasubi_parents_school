Academic Overview

A Strong Foundation for a Brighter Future

At Kasubi Parents Secondary School, we believe that quality education is the key to unlocking every student's potential. Our academic program is carefully structured to provide a strong foundation in the early years of secondary school and specialized, career-focused training at the advanced level.

With an 8:1 student-to-teacher ratio, our educators are able to give each student the individual attention they need to succeed. We use a learner-centered approach that combines rigorous academics with practical application, critical thinking, and character development.

How We Teach

Our Teaching Approach

We combine proven teaching methods with a caring, student-centered philosophy to bring out the best in every learner.

Learner-Centered

Lessons are designed around the needs, abilities, and learning styles of each student.

Mentorship

Every student is assigned a mentor who provides academic guidance and personal support.

Practical Learning

We emphasize hands-on experiments, projects, and real-world applications of knowledge.

Continuous Assessment

Regular tests, mock exams, and feedback sessions keep students on track and improving.
