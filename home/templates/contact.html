Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to some of the most common questions we receive from parents and visitors.

We offer both O-Level (Senior One to Senior Four) and A-Level (Senior Five to Senior Six) education, following the Ugandan national curriculum and preparing students for UNEB examinations.

Yes. We offer both day and boarding options, giving families the flexibility to choose what works best for their child. Our boarding section provides a secure, supportive environment with supervised study and balanced meals.

You can start the admission process by contacting our office, visiting the school in person, or filling out the contact form on this page. Our admissions team will guide you through the requirements and next steps.

Absolutely! We encourage parents and prospective students to visit our campus. Simply call us, email us, or fill out the contact form to book a convenient time for your visit.

We offer a range of A-Level combinations in Sciences (PCB, PCM, BCM, PEM) and Arts (HEG, HEL, HED, EDP), along with subsidiary subjects like General Paper, Subsidiary ICT, and Subsidiary Mathematics.

We welcome partnerships and support in many forms — student sponsorship, infrastructure development, materials donation, mentorship, and more. Please visit our Partnerships page or contact us directly to discuss how you can get involved.
